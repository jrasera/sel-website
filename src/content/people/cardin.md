bio: >
  Associate Professor in Engineering Systems Design at the Dyson School of
  Design Engineering and Director of the Strategic Engineering Laboratory.
  Prior to joining Imperial, he was a faculty member at the National
  University of Singapore (2011–2018), where he established the Strategic
  Engineering Laboratory, and worked as a Quantitative Researcher in the
  hedge fund industry, developing systematic derivatives-trading strategies
  using machine learning. He was a principal investigator for the
  Singapore-ETH Centre Future Resilient Systems project and the
  Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology. He leads the
  development of a global community of academics and practitioners in
  Strategic Engineering, building a repository of open-access materials,
  case studies, analytical models and design tools to help the next
  generation of engineers and project investors build value-enhancing,
  sustainable and resilient infrastructure. He holds a PhD in Engineering
  Systems and an MSc in Technology and Policy from MIT, an MASc in Aerospace
  Engineering from the University of Toronto, an Honours BSc in Physics from
  McGill University, and is a graduate of the Space Science Program at the
  International Space University. He serves as Associate Editor for the
  ASME Journal of Mechanical Design and IISE Transactions, and previously
  served as Associate Editor for the INCOSE journal Systems Engineering
  (2013–2021) and on the Editorial Review Board of IEEE Transactions on
  Engineering Management (2013–2019).
