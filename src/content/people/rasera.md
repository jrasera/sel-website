bio: >
  Mineral processing engineer and Research Associate at Imperial College
  London, working on dry mineral processing technologies for both terrestrial
  applications and lunar in-situ resource utilisation. His research spans dry
  beneficiation, particle segregation, electrostatic separation, dust
  processing and the development of mineral processing systems for the Moon.
  He is also Principal Advisor for Space & Terrestrial Processing at Space RS
  and previously worked as a lunar mineral processing engineer at ispace
  Europe. He holds a PhD in Mineral Processing Engineering from Imperial
  College London, an MSc in Space Studies from the International Space
  University, and MASc and BASc degrees in Mechanical Engineering from the
  University of Waterloo. He has published widely on lunar resource
  processing, electrostatic separation and granular segregation, and has
  contributed to research supported by NASA, ESA, Horizon Europe, the
  Luxembourg National Research Fund and NSERC. He teaches Space Mining at
  Imperial College London, supervises research across mineral processing and
  lunar resources, and serves as Co-Chair of the ISRU UK Representative Group.
