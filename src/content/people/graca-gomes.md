bio: >
  PhD student at the Dyson School of Design Engineering, working on how
  immersive technologies and serious games can support the design and
  policymaking of complex energy systems operating under deep uncertainty.
  Previously Head of Research and Development at the Sino-Portuguese Centre of
  New Energy Technologies — a joint venture between China Three Gorges
  Corporation (CTG) and EDP — where he worked on renewable energy research
  projects between China and Portugal, and before that a renewable energy
  policy analyst at the Portuguese Renewable Energy Association (APREN). He
  holds an MSc in Energy and Environmental Engineering from the University of
  Lisbon and an MEng in Power Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
  He has published widely and contributed to reports for the World Bank, the
  World Energy Council, WindEurope and REN21, and has been recognised by the
  Portuguese Engineers Society and the Institution of Engineering and
  Technology. He sits on the board of the World Energy Council's Future Energy
  Leaders 100 programme and coordinates the Energy, Environment and Blue
  Economy Hub of the Portuguese Overseas Council.
