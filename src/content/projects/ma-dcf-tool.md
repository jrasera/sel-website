overview:
  - >-
    Value chain systems bring together multiple actors, technologies, flows
    and investment decisions. Their development depends on uncertain demand,
    costs, regulation and technology, but also on how different actors
    respond to one another over time.
  - >-
    This project looks at how such systems form and remain viable when
    decisions are distributed across several actors rather than controlled by
    a single decision-maker. It uses modelling and simulation to study how
    investment, participation, timing and coordination shape both individual
    outcomes and the performance of the wider system.
  - >-
    The research also considers how different system architectures,
    development pathways and coordination arrangements perform under
    uncertainty. Initial applications are in the automotive sector, with
    support from Jaguar Land Rover.
