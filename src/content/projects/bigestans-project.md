overview:
  - >-
    This project develops data-driven decision rules for the portfolio
    management of decarbonisation technologies, with carbon capture and reuse
    as the application domain. It is motivated by the need to support
    decarbonisation investment decisions at the level of the portfolio rather
    than the individual technology.
  - >-
    The research has so far applied portfolio and decision-rule approaches to
    direct air capture (DAC) and a range of carbon dioxide removal (CDR)
    technologies. More recently it has added an artificial intelligence layer
    that quantifies the proposed decision-rule method.
