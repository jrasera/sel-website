overview:
  - >-
    This project focuses on In-Situ Resource Utilisation (ISRU) on the Moon,
    optimising the design of lunar resource production plants and their
    deployment strategies. Both are studied under uncertain parameters in the
    lunar environment and in space operations.
  - >-
    The uncertainties considered include physical properties such as the
    particle sizes of the lunar soil and the availability of resources, as
    well as economic ones such as the demand for liquid oxygen on the Moon.
